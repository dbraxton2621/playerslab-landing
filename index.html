QUICK ANSWERS

The questions we get most often, before you ask.

Is this actually free?

Yes. No card, no upsell on the report, no catch. We do this because most players who get a real report eventually want help executing it — and that's when we have a paid conversation. But the report itself is yours either way.

How long does it take?

48 hours from submission to delivery. Sometimes faster. We don't ship reports we haven't fully reviewed, so if we're behind, we'll tell you.

Will you tell me my NIL value in dollars?

No. Anyone who gives you a specific dollar value is making it up. NIL outcomes vary too much by state, school, conference, brand vertical, and timing. We use band labels (Foundation / Building / Ready / Elite) so you know where you stand without anyone making promises that can't be kept.

I'm under 18 — can I still submit?

Yes, but a parent or guardian needs to complete the consent step on the form. We email the report to your parent first, copying you. Standard practice for working with minors in NIL.

What happens after I get the report?

You'll get 3 short emails over the next week — one with the report, one a few days later with our story, and one with the three ways we typically work with players. After that, nothing unless you reach out.

Are you actually an agent?

Players Lab Mgmt is a football-first athlete representation group. We sign 1-3 players a month. Representation is a written agreement, not something we assume from a free report. The report doesn't make us your representative — only a signed agreement does.

What if I'm not at a "name" school?

Good. That's exactly who this is built for. Most of the players we represent are at G5, FCS, or buried on a Power-4 depth chart. The whole point is that the market undervalues them — and the report is what closes that gap.
